Control your Homey Pro directly from your Rabbit R1 using this dedicated integration. This app allows you to send voice commands and receive feedback in a beautiful, colorful interface designed specifically for the R1 display.

Setup is quick and easy: once the app is installed on your Homey, go to the App Settings to find the QR Code for the Rabbit R1 'Creation' installation and the secondary QR Code to pair your device and start controlling your home.
